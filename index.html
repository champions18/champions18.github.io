Signals designed for mule-account behavior.

Instead of relying on a single suspicious transaction, the application scores accounts by combining behavioral patterns, identity attributes, graph relationships, and investigator feedback.

01

Transaction velocity

Detect sudden inbound-payment bursts, immediate outward transfers, round-value cash-outs, and fast balance depletion.

02

Network graph risk

Map accounts, beneficiaries, devices, phone numbers, IPs, merchants, and ATMs to expose shared infrastructure.

03

Onboarding anomalies

Flag synthetic identity indicators, recycled contact details, risky document signals, and dormant-to-active account shifts.

04

Explainable scoring

Give analysts the reason codes, comparable historical typologies, and confidence level behind every alert.

05

Case orchestration

Route high-risk cases to investigators with evidence timelines, account actions, and SAR/STR note templates.

06

Feedback learning

Use confirmed mule, false-positive, and escalated outcomes to tune thresholds and improve future prioritization.

From event stream to investigator decision.

The design supports a controlled bank environment where models assist trained staff rather than replacing compliance judgment.

Ingest

Stream core banking events, payment rails, KYC records, digital telemetry, and negative-news/watchlist indicators.

Score

Apply rules, anomaly detection, supervised models, and graph features to produce an account-level mule risk score.

Investigate

Present a case timeline, relationship graph, reason codes, peer comparisons, and recommended next-best actions.

Act

Trigger holds, enhanced due diligence, customer outreach, law-enforcement packages, or no-action closures with audit trails.

Prioritize the cases that matter most.
